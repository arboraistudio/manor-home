# Manor Product Facts

Manor is a shared living mobile app by Arbor AI Studio. It is built for roommates, couples, families, and shared households that need one place to coordinate household responsibilities.

## What Manor Does

Manor helps households:

- Track shared expenses in a smart ledger.
- Calculate who owes whom after shared purchases.
- Assign chores with deadlines and point values.
- Keep a real-time shared shopping list.
- Turn shopping items into ledger expenses.
- Keep household updates in a shared hub.
- Use light and dark themes.

## Who Manor Is For

Manor is for people who share a home and want less friction around everyday household logistics. Common audiences include roommate groups, couples, families, student housing, and other shared households.

## Primary Use Cases

- Replacing spreadsheets for roommate expenses.
- Reducing repeated reminders about chores.
- Splitting household purchases clearly.
- Coordinating shopping before someone goes to the store.
- Keeping shared household information out of scattered group chats.

## Positioning

Manor is not a banking app, payment processor, landlord portal, property management system, or generic team task manager. Manor is focused on the recurring coordination problems inside a shared home.

## Manor Compared With Common Alternatives

| Household need | Manor | Spreadsheet or whiteboard | Group chat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shared expenses | Smart ledger calculates who owes whom after shared purchases. | Must be updated by hand. | Costs get buried in messages. |
| Chore accountability | Assignments, deadlines, and points live in one app. | Goes stale unless someone maintains it. | Reminders become repeated nagging. |
| Shopping coordination | Real-time list syncs household items and can flow into the ledger. | Does not update when someone is already at the store. | Duplicate purchases happen when messages are missed. |
| Best fit | Roommates, couples, families, and shared homes that want one source of truth. | Very small households with rare shared purchases. | Quick one-off coordination. |
